Week #1
Clash - Colors or patterns that look ugly together.
Winking - Blinking one eye at someone.
Suggest - Give ideas or plans for someone to think about.
Scrunches - Squeezed or crushed into a different shape.
Mushy - Soft and squishy.
Usual - The way something often is.
Bilingual - Able to speak more than one language.
Mismatched - Things that do not fit or do not belong together.

Week #2
Moody - Change feelings often and quickly.
Pesky - Something that is annoying.
Illustrate - Draw pictures that go with the story.
Snarled - Twisted and tangled.
Annual - Happens once a year.
Recited - Said aloud after learning.
Protested - Said why you do not agree with a statement or idea.
